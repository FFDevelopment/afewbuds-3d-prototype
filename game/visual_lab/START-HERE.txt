AFewBuds Visual Lab 01.6 — Muted wood, basement grow controls and tent placement

OPEN THIS AS A SEPARATE PROJECT
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a new folder (do not copy over your game).
2. In Godot Project Manager, Import the included project.godot.
3. Let the asset import finish, then press F5 to run the project.
   Once the game is running, F6 toggles original/revised visuals.

Godot: validated with Godot 4.6 stable, Compatibility renderer.
The source snapshot declares 4.7; this copy was imported and checked with 4.6.

CONTROLS
WASD / mouse: walk / look. Shift: sprint. E: interact/open doors.
Esc: pause or release mouse. P: existing phone.
F6: switch revised / original materials and added details.
F7: Desktop / Light rendering preset (MSAA and shadow distance).
F8: afternoon / late afternoon lighting.
F9: cycle apartment exterior, market, living room, fire escape,
    front-door wall, east residences, police station, house exterior,
    house living room, basement, and rooftop.
B while in basement: toggle basement lights.
F5 while running: existing local save action.

BASEMENT ACCESS
Enter the house, go through the rear-right grow-room doorway, and turn
right to the stair opening. Walk down and back up; F9 also offers a
basement inspection view. The eight fixed cultivation platforms are removed.
Place your own tents/equipment using the existing Backpack/Arrange controls.
The main basement floor accepts grow equipment; the east stair/panel aisle
and the service strip along the tanks/counter remain reserved.
Existing upstairs furniture stays put. Empty and unlock tents before moving
or packing them, using the same rules as before. No purchases or plants are reset.
House equipment keeps its original property ID, plant slots and inventory.
Basement furniture, plants and interactions use and save the lower floor height.
The east-wall grow panel downstairs opens with E and uses the apartment-style
status/control screen. Room Light controls basement ceiling lights; Grow Lights
and Ventilation control HOUSE equipment on both floors, separately from apartment
controls. Ventilation requires a placed house unit. B also toggles basement lights.
Worker navigation through the new basement has not been validated.
The basement structure stays present when F6 compares the art pass.

WHAT THIS IS
Revision 01.6: continuous railing barriers prevent stair-exit snagging on
the fire escape and basement stairs.

Wood/basement update: coordinated matte oak floors and wooden doors, including the
apartment floor. Wood grain stays attached to swinging doors. The house panel
now opens a full control screen downstairs. Removed fixed cultivation benches
and added height-aware player equipment placement, rendering and saved positions.

Earlier revision 01.4: supported market board and awning, corrected awning slope,
crate/board collision; roof perimeter and upper landing rails with collision.
Indoor checks now include height, preventing apartment door alerts on the
roof. House room controls also respect floor height. Stair-connected house
basement with a cut through both the actual floor and terrain collision.
An early environment art direction study on the actual current map.
Apartment proportions: roof cornice lifted clear of the top windows; two equally
spaced 3.5 m upper stories, larger upper windows, aligned floor bands,
and extended pilasters/downpipes. The playable ground floor stays intact.

Revised masonry, pavement/asphalt, interior plaster, warm light, entrance
canopy, layered cornice, drainpipes, side fire escape, market awning and
small street props. Character models and gameplay are from the snapshot.
Revision 01.3: restored transparent glass and original window frames on the
enterable house; narrowed facade material selection to actual wall pieces.
Roof sheets are visible from both sides, and new soffits close the eave gap.
Original house wood floors are preserved. Eleven inspection viewpoints.
Compatibility light budget increased from 8 to 16 per object: the house
floor and ceiling overlap 11 local lights (including the room lights). This avoids camera-dependent
light selection on those large meshes. This is a desktop preview setting;
phone performance remains unmeasured.

Revision 01.2: consistent world-scaled brick, concrete, asphalt, gravel,
soil, lawn and plaster across the neighborhood. Apartment front-door
wall fragments now share the same plaster as the remaining walls.
Background window glass, frames and sills enlarged together by 15%.
Three stair flights with solid connected landings now lead from the
ground to the 11.6 m roof; handrails and smooth ramp collision included.

Original mode restores the original materials and hides the added props;
the review time and rendering preset remain fixed for comparison.

LIMITS
This is not a completed Schedule I-quality makeover or a mobile build.
The Light preset is an experiment, not a measured phone performance result.
The fire escape has collision; small decorative props remain visual only.
Existing furniture, characters and trees retain their authored assets.
Background buildings remain closed shells; no new playable interiors.
