# Windows playtest checklist

- Movement feels comfortable; no clipping through walls, bench, couch, or storage.
- Both rooms are reachable through the grow-room doorway.
- E prompts align with the intended object and disappear beyond reach.
- Harvest a ready plant, use the packaging bench, and store the result.
- Walk to product storage, supply shelf, and system terminal; each opens the correct UI.
- P opens the phone. Mouse is free in menus, movement stops, and closing restores mouse look.
- Esc pauses. Returning does not move the character to a fixed camera anchor.
- Save with F5, exit, reopen, and verify inventory and location.
- Check 1280×800 and 1920×1080 fullscreen/window resizing for clipped menus.

Not acceptance criteria for 0.1: physical grab/place, new NPC navigation, mobile touch walking, city exploration, live cloud saves, or dealer/backpack inventory rebuild.
