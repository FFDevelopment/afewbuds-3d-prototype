Chapters 5–15

Chapter 5 has local mechanics. Chapters 6–15 below are a roadmap, not a claim of playable content.

CHAPTER 5 · Roachwood / Half Baked Heights

Building an Operation

Rod helps turn the new house into a stable operation. Kobi tests batch consistency; Malik asks the player to keep the crew dependable.

  1. Make It Yours: place and lock two ordinary furniture items in the house.
  2. Room to Grow: place a tent in the grow room and harvest three new plants.
  3. A Consistent Batch: trim 30g and seal five bags.
  4. House Regulars: five personal sales and two dealer sales after moving.
  5. Keep the Lights On: pay a house utility bill and clear overdue property balances.
  6. Open House, Quiet Business: earn $2,500 new revenue, make ten personal sales, lower Heat to 25 or less, and meet Rod.

Finale / unlock: Rod recognizes the house as an established operation and introduces the Joint Junction opportunity. $1,600 total milestone rewards; each reward is granted once.

CHAPTER 6 · Joint Junction

A Place at the Junction

Diddy introduces the downtown regulars. The player learns to manage appointments without overwhelming the house.

  1. Meet Diddy downtown after Chapter 5.
  2. Complete three scheduled pickups for existing friends.
  3. Set a storefront schedule and honor five appointments.
  4. Choose a small service counter or appointment-only office; both advance the story.
  5. Pay the first agreement bill and complete the opening day.

Finale / unlock: Unlock a second operating location and appointment scheduling upgrades. Choice controls location flavor, not access to later chapters.

CHAPTER 7 · Cottonmouth Commons / Munchie Mile

The Regulars

Tyler and Jeremias disagree about chasing more customers versus looking after regulars. Reliability wins over raw volume.

  1. Introduce Tyler and Jeremias through their existing friend relationships.
  2. Reach a dependable relationship with four named friends.
  3. Complete three small requests with different time windows.
  4. Resolve one missed appointment through a make-good rather than losing the chapter.
  5. Finish a weekend of ten fulfilled orders.

Finale / unlock: Unlock customer notes, repeat orders, and a staff scheduling board.

CHAPTER 8 · Couchlock Court

A Crew of Your Own

Malik needs the player to stop being the answer to every problem. Roles and fair pay become the center of the story.

  1. Assign three distinct staff roles.
  2. Train one worker through five supervised tasks.
  3. Cover a day off by reassigning duties.
  4. Pay three payroll cycles without overdue wages.
  5. Resolve a staff disagreement and finish a fully staffed shift.

Finale / unlock: Unlock shift presets, training levels, and a deputy manager; no required firing route.

CHAPTER 9 · Bongwater Bay

Room for the Boxes

Rod finds a modest warehouse. The challenge is organizing the move while keeping customers supplied.

  1. Inspect a warehouse and choose rent, lease, or purchase.
  2. Arrange receiving, storage, and packing areas with clear access.
  3. Move owned equipment without losing contents or upgrades.
  4. Complete five delivery collections within carrying capacity.
  5. Run one day with correct stock and paid utilities.

Finale / unlock: Unlock warehouse ownership agreements, larger furniture, and property-to-property logistics.

CHAPTER 10 · Cottonmouth Commons

Your Name on the Bag

Kobi wants a recognizable house style. Quality and honest labeling matter more than charging extra for a faster bench.

  1. Choose a brand name and visual identity.
  2. Complete three consistent quality batches.
  3. Record stock and labels for five orders.
  4. Run a small launch for existing regulars.
  5. Resolve one quality complaint with a refund or replacement.

Finale / unlock: Unlock cosmetic packaging and a brand ledger; bench upgrades remain speed/convenience upgrades.

CHAPTER 11 · Paranoia Point

Too Much Attention

The growing business draws scrutiny. Reeves returns as a character with consequences shaped by the earlier payment choice.

  1. Review the prior Reeves outcome without reviving retired tasks.
  2. Receive a district notice and inspect the affected property.
  3. Reduce noise/traffic through schedules and equipment settings.
  4. Resolve bills and complete three quiet shifts.
  5. Choose a lawful settlement or temporary closure; both converge at the finale.

Finale / unlock: Unlock compliance and property alerts. No real-world evasion instructions; pressure stays abstract and fictional.

CHAPTER 12 · High Society Hills

Higher Expectations

Rod introduces a demanding client circle. The player must decide what premium service actually means.

  1. Visit High Society Hills with an existing friend.
  2. Furnish a reception space with accessible seating.
  3. Meet three customer preference requests.
  4. Complete five on-time appointments.
  5. Host a small invitation-only opening with a staffed reception.

Finale / unlock: Unlock premium decor, client preference profiles, and service tiers without automatic product-value inflation.

CHAPTER 13 · The Sticks

Out Past the Lights

Mahto brings a rural property lead. Distance and utilities make the new site a different kind of commitment.

  1. Tour two rural property options.
  2. Choose an agreement and budget for recurring obligations.
  3. Install an appropriate growing room and utility equipment.
  4. Assign on-site staff and test a transport route.
  5. Complete the first rural production cycle.

Finale / unlock: Unlock rural properties, larger capacity equipment, and scheduled transport. Earlier homes remain optional holdings.

CHAPTER 14 · Multiple districts

One Business, Many Doors

The player has several places but needs one coherent operation. Malik and the crew help build routines that survive a busy week.

  1. Assign a manager to each active operation.
  2. Balance stock between three controlled properties.
  3. Cover five shifts with no missed payroll.
  4. Resolve a shortage through transfer or reduced orders.
  5. Finish a multi-property week with all bills accounted for.

Finale / unlock: Unlock a city-wide operations overview, transfer plans, and manager summaries.

CHAPTER 15 · Bongchester

Bongchester Built Together

The friends return for the city story finale. The ending reflects relationships, retained properties, and earlier choices.

  1. Complete personal follow-up scenes with the core friends.
  2. Resolve outstanding story obligations; old debt cannot vanish.
  3. Choose the final business identity: neighborhood roots, service network, or independent craft.
  4. Prepare and host a celebration at a property the player controls.
  5. Review the completed city story and choose free-play goals.

Finale / unlock: Complete the ten-chapter continuation with a persistent epilogue, optional challenges, and no forced reset.
